# Shipping

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days inside the continental United States. Expedited shipping takes 1 to 2 business days. The expedited shipping cost is a flat $18 per order, not per item.

Orders placed before 1pm Eastern ship the same day. Later orders ship the next business day. We ship to the United States and Canada. We do not ship to PO boxes.

A tracking number is emailed when the carrier scans the label. The usual carrier is Northline Freight. Freight sofas move on the standard schedule only, and expedited shipping is not offered for those large pieces.
