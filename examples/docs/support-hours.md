# Support hours

Customer support is open Monday through Friday, 9am to 6pm Eastern. Saturday support is open from 10am to 2pm Eastern. The Saturday support close time is 2pm Eastern. There is no Saturday support after 2pm.

We are closed on Sundays and on US federal holidays. Support appointments are video calls in 30 minute slots during open hours. The support email is help@harborandco.example.

Phone support uses the same hours as the inbox. Holiday closures are posted on the contact page the week before.
