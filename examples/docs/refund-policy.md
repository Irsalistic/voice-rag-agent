# Refund policy

Harbor & Co. accepts a return within 30 days of delivery. The item must be unused and in its original packaging. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 business days after the warehouse receives the return.

Shipping fees are non-refundable unless the return is our error. Opened candles are not refundable. Custom upholstery is final sale. Bring the packing slip or the order id when you start a return.

The returns desk emails a prepaid label for defective goods. For a change of mind, the customer pays the return postage. Refund timing counts business days only, and weekends do not count toward those 5 business days.
