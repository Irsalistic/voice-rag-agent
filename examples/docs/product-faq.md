# Product FAQ

The Oakline sofa is made of kiln-dried hardwood and performance linen. It seats three adults. The seat height is 18 inches. The linen cover is removable, and a steam cleaner is safe on that cover.

The Halden lamp uses an LED bulb rated for 25000 hours. The shade is frosted glass. Wipe the frosted glass shade with a dry cloth. Do not use an abrasive cleaner on the brass base. The lamp is 26 inches tall.

Replacement linen covers for the Oakline sofa ship separately. The Halden lamp bulb is not a standard household bulb. Order a replacement from the parts desk.
